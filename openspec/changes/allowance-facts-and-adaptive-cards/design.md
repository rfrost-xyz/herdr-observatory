## Account instruments

The account card presents the weekly remaining share as its principal measurement. Reset time, nonzero reset passes, expiry detail and observation age stay visible or accessible. It never derives a depletion time or pace from the seven-day window. A stale or expired sample retains an unknown balance and does not imply a refill. React and fallback paths share the same validated view model.

## Thread cards

The card consists of identity, activity, metric tiles and provenance modules. Their content sets the row's minimum height. The thread grid scrolls inside the available area when two rows cannot fit. Narrow layouts use content-sized rows and keep the document's horizontal edge intact. The metric tiles remain labelled and use existing bounded values and accessible details.
