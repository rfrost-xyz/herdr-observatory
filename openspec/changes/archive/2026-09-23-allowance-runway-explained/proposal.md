## Why

The displayed Burn and Room rates do not directly answer how long the reported allowance remaining might last. With 10% left, a user has to divide that percentage by Burn themselves.

## What changes

Show a compact estimated runway beside the reported weekly percentage. Calculate it from the remaining percentage and the observed whole-window average burn. Keep the reset time and rate bars visible, and make the estimate's assumption clear. Unknown or stale inputs yield an unknown estimate.

## Impact

The account card and its documentation change. No new account data, transport fields or authentication permissions are required.
