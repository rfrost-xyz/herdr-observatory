## Why

An average-burn runway changes sharply with the number of active agents and implies a reliability the account source cannot provide. Burn and Room repeat information already contained in the remaining balance and reset time. Thread cards also clip their lower metrics when their content exceeds a fixed grid row.

## What changes

- Show the reported weekly share, scheduled reset, available reset passes and sample age without a projected depletion time or derived pace charts.
- Let thread card modules determine their minimum height, with scrolling confined to the thread area when the viewport is short.
- Keep technical detail readable in compact modules, including source age and retained usage.

## Capabilities

### Modified capabilities

- `account-allowances`: present account facts without derived forecasts or pace instruments.
- `activity-dashboard`: keep every visible card module reachable across thread states and viewport sizes.

## Impact

Browser view model, React and fallback renderers, CSS, presentation tests and canonical specifications. Account collection, sharing and session telemetry are unchanged.
