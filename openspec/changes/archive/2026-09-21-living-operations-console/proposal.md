# Living operations console

## Why
Literal heartbeat strips and standard cards do not convey the requested living technical console.

## What Changes
Replace strips with whole-card circuit, scan and texture effects; rebuild the screen as an operations console with a derived snapshot command-line feed and continuously moving state-driven surfaces.

## Capabilities
### Modified Capabilities
- office-display: state-driven console presentation.

## Impact
Browser and README only. Preserve source filtering, truthful metadata, no-scroll geometry and reduced motion.
