# Proposal

## Why

The deficit should read as missing allowance, while particles and glow celebrate a surplus. A signed pace reading makes the difference explicit.

## What Changes

- Show a same-accent hatched deficit from actual remaining allowance to the expected tick.
- Keep the thin surplus interval; restrict hover particles and glow to positive pace.
- Add a one-decimal signed percentage beside the remaining percentage, with percentage-point meaning explained on hover.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `omarchy-companion`: Allowance deficit texture, signed pace and positive-only effects.

## Impact

Local plugin presentation and focused fixtures. Preserve the existing worktree, collector, account privacy and remote services. No Git history or forge changes.
