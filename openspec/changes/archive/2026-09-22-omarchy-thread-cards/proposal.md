## Why
The CLI-dominated presentation no longer matches the desired direction. An Omarchy-inspired composition should give threads the greatest prominence, readable fleet information and activity driven by real harness events.

## What Changes
- Replace the full-screen terminal with a compact header, fleet strip and prominent thread cards.
- Adapt the music activity motif into a clearly labelled observed-agent activity indicator that settles when quiet.
- Give each card bounded state/hook impulses, readable phase/tool/context details and ordinary Herdr state names.
- Use the synchronised OS palette throughout surfaces, text, borders and status accents.
- Keep occasional text effects only in a small separate recent-activity area, never on cards.

## Capabilities
### Modified Capabilities
- `office-display`: Card-focused one-screen composition, labelled fleet metrics, event-driven activity header and theme coverage.

## Impact
Browser presentation and tests, README, and the two existing Docker images. Reuse collectors, privacy filtering, hooks and theme transport; no new daemon, dependency or OS configuration.
